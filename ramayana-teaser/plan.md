# RAMAYANA: wordless motion teaser (director's plan)

Made with the `motion-director` skill. No reference videos, no photographs. The characters are drawn and rigged in code. The only word on screen is the title.

## 1. Insight

- **Tension:** one steady line of dharma, against ego that multiplies.
- **Meaning words found in the epic itself:** the bow (Kodanda); the *Lakshman Rekha*, a protective line literally drawn on the ground; Ravana's ten heads; Hanuman's leap across the ocean; the bridge of stones; the Sun dynasty (Rama is Suryavanshi).
- **Takeaway:** the epic, retold with power and devotion. **Action:** remember the title.

## 2. Concepts

| | Concept | Idea | Motion metaphor |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | **One Line** | A single golden line is the path of dharma. It draws the bow, becomes the Lakshman Rekha, breaks when Ravana's ego multiplies, arcs as Hanuman's leap, becomes the bridge, flies as the arrow, and closes into the sun. | A continuous drawn line that changes role at every beat |
| B | Ten vs One | Ten heads multiply on the beat until one arrow ends them | Multiplication against a single vector |
| C | Shadow puppets | The epic as a traditional leather-puppet play behind a firelit screen | Hinged puppet silhouettes |

**A wins.** The line *is* a symbol in the Ramayana itself, it gives every character a reason to appear, and it closes naturally into the Sun dynasty's sun for the title. B lives inside A as the Ravana beat.

## 3. Ending

The shattered fragments of Ravana's heads converge into one golden ring, which becomes the sun: the disc fills, the rays bloom, and the concentric rings from the opening return. **RAMAYANA** rises letter by letter in a carved serif, and the line underlines it. Hold about 1.5 s while the sun breathes.

## 4. Tempo and structure

96 BPM, so 1 beat = 0.625 s and 1 bar = 2.5 s. 15 s is exactly 6 bars, 16:9 at 1920×1080, 60 fps.

Contrast curve: `still tension (bow drawn) | sacred calm (Sita, Rekha) | violent burst (10 heads on 16ths) | flight → FIRE flip | build (stones on 16ths) → release → impact | resolve and hold`

## 6. Continuity

- **Persistent motif:** the golden line. It is the bow, then the ground, the Rekha, the leap trail, the bridge, the arrow's path, and finally the sun.
- **Callback:** Rama's drawn bow from shot 1 releases in shot 5. The setup is the tension; the payoff is the strike.
- **Bookend:** concentric sun rings and a conch at the open, the full sun and the conch at the close. The score moves from D minor drone to D major resolution.
