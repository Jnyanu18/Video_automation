[excited] Your Wi-Fi and your phone hotspot can download at the same time, and it's free.

It's called net-merge. It adds up every connection you have, so your downloads use all of them at once.

The developer's test: Wi-Fi, an Android phone and an iPhone, nine downloads running. 232 to 303 megabits. The fastest link alone? About 121.

It works with any app and doesn't need an account. You install it on Windows, Mac or Linux, and a tray icon shows each connection's speed.

One catch. A single download still rides a single link, so it shines when lots of things download at once, like game updates and torrents.
