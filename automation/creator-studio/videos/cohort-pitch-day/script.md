On Saturday I walked into a room full of founders with one thing: a six minute pitch.

No slides.

I pitched AskVerb to a founder who has already raised a Series B. I was given ten minutes. We talked for fifteen.

He liked it.

[excited] And I made the next round: Leap by Masai's Founders' Cohort. It starts in Goa and ends in Bengaluru, pitching to investors.

[softly] But I can't go.

I don't have the money for it right now. Still, I left with something better than a seat.

His advice.

Don't build for every SaaS yet. Start where the workflow is so complex that only a few people know how it works, and nothing is written down.

That's my first niche.

[warmly] I heard from VCs and founders, met people building all kinds of things, and that same day, four people signed up. My second best day so far.

[confident] Day 26. Still going.
