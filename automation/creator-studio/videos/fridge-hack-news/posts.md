## Instagram

Hackers reportedly turned supermarket fridges up to fifty degrees, and how they got in is almost boring.

On Monday 2 November, apparent cyberattacks hit supermarket fridges in seven countries: Belgium, Sweden, the Netherlands, Estonia, Luxembourg, Finland and Ireland. In October the same kind of attack hit hundreds of French stores, and Intermarché's owner put the losses at several million euros. Danfoss, whose fridge software was behind both, says the stores hit were running outdated versions.

The same weekend, Germany's cyber agency told lawmakers to treat their iPhones as possibly hacked after Apple's spyware warnings, and the emergency alert apps in Ukraine and Russia both sent fake nuclear warnings.

Nobody has claimed the fridge attacks, and no report has linked these events.
