# Brief: 25 steps, 4 screens, one sentence

- **Brand:** personal (Vishnu), AskVerb.
- **Funnel:** middle to bottom (AskVerb). Posted as a LinkedIn video first; the same beats become a 7-slide PDF carousel a few days later.
- **Who it is for:** SaaS teams who already want an AI agent inside their product that does things, and do not want to build the part that makes it safe (target set 2026-11-05, docs/54 section 2).
- **The one point:** the hard part of an in-product agent is not the AI, it is making it safe to act: it acts as the signed-in user, shows what will change before it does, and logs everything.
- **The hook (first 5 seconds):** "Twenty-five steps across four screens, just to get one thing done." A tall stack of pixel screens, a counter reading 25 STEPS, change of screen by 1 s.
- **Shape and length:** about 32 s, 9:16, eight beats. A long line, then a short one.
- **The metaphor or visual idea:** a step counter. It climbs to 17 / 25 while Ava slumps in a maze of dialogs, then drops to 1 / 1 when the user types one sentence. The confirm card is the centre: the real card's wording ("Needs your approval", Cancel, Confirm), drawn in pixel art.
- **Look:** 16-bit pixel art on black, brand gold #ffc400, white, one green (#2f7a43) for done. Ava big at the bottom with a pose on every beat. Not the dark liquid glass default: Vishnu chose the pixel look on 2026-11-06 for this one. No 3D.
- **Real numbers it uses, and where they come from:** "25 steps, 4 screens" is an illustration, not a customer figure. The example change on the card (a delivery moved to a new slot) is invented and generic. No customer, post or founder is quoted.
- **Facts on the confirm card:** frontend/widget/src/panel.ts: eyebrow "Needs your approval", a title, rows of label and value, buttons Cancel and Confirm.
- **Ends on:** "Give your app an agent that gets work done", with askverb.com/demos. No call ask.
- **Music:** a library track (chiptune-leaning if one exists), Vishnu picks. No new generated music.
