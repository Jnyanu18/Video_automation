# Brief: Why you should still hire an agency in the age of Claude

- **Brand:** agency (Auralion Labs), Vishnu to camera.
- **Who it is for:** non-technical founders who have just raised money, deciding whether to build with AI tools
  themselves or hire a team (the buyer in the founders' client plan, 2026-11).
- **The one point:** AI gets you a prototype; a product people rely on needs a team that owns production,
  judgment and the work after launch. If yours doesn't need that, use Claude.
- **Purple cow:** an agency founder admitting that many founders shouldn't hire an agency. Shows at: the first
  line ("half the founders we talk to ask why they shouldn't just build it with Claude"), then "Keep your money."
  Pays off at: the rule near the end, which tells some viewers to use Claude.
- **Loops:** A+ L1 "why not just Claude?" / B+ L3 "the prototype works on their laptop" / B- L4 "Claude won't
  pick up" / C+ L5 "the second thing" (judgment, then time) / C- L6 / A- L7 the rule.
- **Shape and length:** talking head, 9:16, about 75 seconds, nine short paragraphs.
- **Real numbers, and where each comes from:** none. Every claim is a general one he can stand behind; no client
  stories or stats invented.
- **The question it ends on:** none; it ends on an offer to tell the viewer which one they need.
