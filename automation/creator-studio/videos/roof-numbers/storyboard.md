## storm | After the worst hurricane
chapter: The storm
Flat cartoon, a coastal street at dusk after the storm: bent palms, shingles on the road, tarps. One roofer
finishing one roof. A street sign: Palm St.

## one | Just one.
Wide shot: one neat roof in a row of wrecked ones.

## codes | Before he left
He sticks a QR code on each corner of the roof, facing the street, one, two, three, four.

## laugh | "Who scans a roof?"
Two neighbors at the fence, one points up and laughs. The roofer packs his truck, unbothered.

## morning | But by morning
chapter: The morning
Sunrise. Neighbors on their porches with phones to their ears. Over each one, a small grey bubble:
"Voicemail".

## nobody | Nobody was picking up.
Close on one neighbor lowering the phone, tired.

## scan | Then someone looked across the road
He looks up, sees the four squares across the road. Phone raised, the camera frame locks onto a code with
green corner brackets.

## next | Then the house next door.
Phones rise on porch after porch along the block, each one locking on.

## ring | Every scan rang that one roofer's phone
The roofer at the top of a ladder, both hands full of shingles, phone buzzing in his pocket again and again.

## never | He never touched it.
Close on the buzzing pocket. His hands keep working.

## booked | But every call got answered
Back on the porches: faces change as someone picks up. Green "Booked" tags pop over the houses one by one,
until the whole street is tagged. A clock shows 11:40.

## who | So who picked up?
chapter: Ava
The camera dives into one neighbor's phone screen.

## ava | Ava.
Hard switch to the site look: pale dotted stage, a white call card, the orange dot, "Answered by Ava",
a soft waveform. Then the Auralion Labs mark with a mono label: "AI RECEPTIONIST".

## every | Every call. Every job.
Two white pills land on their words: "Every call", "Every job".

## end | Your roof doesn't need QR codes.
The end card in the site style: "Your phone needs Ava." Ava's line +1 (415) 358-6725 on the dark button,
auralionlabs.com under it. "Auralion Labs" lands on the last line.
