## YouTube Short

Title: Google won't let you publish your app until 12 people test it for 14 days

Description:
Google won't let you publish your app until 12 people keep it on their phones for 14 days.

If you made a personal Google Play developer account after 13 November 2023, you have to run a closed test before you can go live. Internal testing doesn't count. Twelve testers have to opt in and keep the app installed for 14 days in a row, and you can watch the count on your Play Console dashboard. Only then can you apply for production, and Google can take up to a week to review it.

Two ways through: line up your 12 testers before you finish building, not after. Or register as an organisation with a D-U-N-S number, and the rule doesn't apply.

I've published two apps on Google Play, and this is the rule nobody warns you about. Narrated by my cloned voice, reading a script I wrote and approved.
