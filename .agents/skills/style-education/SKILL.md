# Style Guide: Modern Educational & Aptitude Masterclasses

Use this style profile whenever creating an educational video, competitive exam tutorial, or mathematics explainer reel.

## 1. Visual Framing & Layout
- **Aspect Ratio:** Vertical 9:16 (1080 × 1920) @ 30 FPS.
- **Background:** Deep obsidian-navy (`#070B19`) with soft radial ambient glow (`#111D38`).
- **Top Header:** Clean branded category pill (`APTITUDE MASTERCLASS`) with a dynamic 60s progress bar.
- **Safe Area:** Main educational animations positioned in the middle 70% of the screen.

## 2. Typography & Color Palette
- **Palette:**
  - Base: Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) on Frosted Dark Slate (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.85)`).
  - Primary Highlight: Electric Cyan (`#00E5FF`) for titles, tags, and active grid blocks.
  - Keyword Accent: Bright Yellow (`#FDE047`) for formulas, key terms, and rules.
  - Final Answers: Emerald Green (`#10B981`) for correct results, calculations, and CTA buttons.
  - Negatives / Discounts: Crimson Red (`#EF4444`) for discount tags and price drops.
- **Font:** Clean modern geometric sans-serif (Inter / System Bold Display).

## 3. Synchronized Captions
- **Position:** Floating lower-third pill at `bottom: 160px` (safely clear of mobile app UI).
- **Styling:** Word-by-word synchronized captions timed via Whisper.
- **Color Coding:** Yellow for technical terms, green for numbers and answers, white for body dialogue.

## 4. Voiceover & Audio Design
- **Voice:** Natural articulate educator voice (`en-US-ChristopherNeural` or engaging clear tone).
- **Pacing:** ~140–150 WPM (energetic but clear and easy to follow).
- **Music:** Subtle study / lo-fi electric piano instrumental kept at -20dB under the voice.
- **Sound Effects:**
  - Gentle pop/clicks on card reveals and grid cells.
  - Soft chime / ding on final answers and eureka moments.
  - Subtle whoosh on mathematical transformations.
