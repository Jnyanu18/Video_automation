# Style Guide: Clean, Editorial Fashion & Luxury Reels

Use this style profile whenever editing for fashion brands, luxury apparel, fragrance, jewelry, or aesthetic lifestyle content.

## 1. Visual Framing & Pacing
- **Aspect Ratio:** Vertical 9:16 (1080 × 1920) @ 30 FPS.
- **Pacing:** Slow, elegant, deliberate cuts (2.5s – 4.5s average shot length).
- **Movement:** Subtle, continuous slow push-ins/zooms (`scale 1.0x` to `1.08x`), gentle whip-glides, and seamless cross-dissolves.
- **Color Grading:** Film-emulation aesthetic:
  - Warm golden-hour tones or muted editorial desaturation.
  - Soft contrast, lifted shadow blacks, and controlled creamy highlights.
  - Subtle, fine 35mm film grain texture.

## 2. Typography & Motion Graphics (HyperFrames)
- **Palette:**
  - Backgrounds: Muted tones — Charcoal Black (`#121212`), Warm Bone (`#F7F5F0`), or Champagne Gold (`#E6D5B8`).
  - Text: Clean Alabaster White (`#FAFAFA`) with Warm Champagne (`#D6C29E`) accents.
- **Typography:**
  - Primary Display: High-contrast luxury serif (`Playfair Display`, `Bodoni`, `Cinzel`, or `Cormorant Garamond`).
  - Secondary / Detail: Tracking-expanded modern sans (`Montserrat`, `Neue Montreal`, or `Satoshi`, letter-spacing: `0.25em`).
- **Graphic Elements:**
  - Minimalist thin divider lines (`1px`).
  - Elegant lower-third brand pills with smooth opacity fades.
  - Editorial magazine split-screens (top/bottom or asymmetric fashion grids).

## 3. Audio & Sound Design
- **Soundtrack:** Deep chic house, slow French indie electronic, or warm vinyl lofi.
- **Sound Design:**
  - Soft, airy whooshes (never harsh EDM risers or loud sub-booms).
  - Subtle fabric rustles, gentle camera shutter clicks, and soft vinyl crackle texture.
  - Audio kept smooth with gentle cross-fades between shots.
