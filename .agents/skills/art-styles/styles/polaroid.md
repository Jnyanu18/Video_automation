# Polaroid / Scrapbook

Instant photos taped into a scrapbook, with handwritten captions and sticky notes.

Reference tiles: `scrapbook` in `remotion/src/lanes/styles/StyleSheet.tsx` (a headline, a chat and a stat). Build from them.

- **Colours:** Paper `#EFE6D8`, white photo frames, ink `#2a2018`, red `#C2452D`, sticky yellow `#FFF59D`, index-card lines `#c8d8ee`.
- **Type:** Playfair Display 900 for the headline, Caveat 700 for captions and notes.
- **Shapes and texture:** The `Polaroid` frame (thick bottom border with the caption, tilted, shadow), tape on corners, notes on index cards and sticky notes, grain at 0.25.
- **Motion:** Photos drop and settle with a small rotate; captions write on.
- **Use for:** Personal story, day 1 versus day 24, behind the scenes.
- **Avoid:** Fake photos: only real photos of him or the product.
