# Sticker bomb

A laptop lid covered in die-cut stickers.

Reference tiles: `stickers` in `remotion/src/lanes/styles/StyleSheet.tsx` (a headline, a chat and a stat). Build from them.

- **Colours:** Laptop grey `#2a2a30` with a fine dot texture; stickers in yellow `#FFD23F`, purple `#7B5CFF`, green `#3DDC97`, pink `#FF4F79`, or black with acid green text; every sticker has a 7px white die-cut border.
- **Type:** Bungee for shouty stickers, Fredoka for the softer ones.
- **Shapes and texture:** Each phrase is its own sticker, tilted -12 to 14 degrees, with a drop shadow `0 6px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.45)`; small extras (a star, "GO!", the URL) round the edges.
- **Motion:** Stickers slap on one by one with overshoot and a peel sound.
- **Use for:** Hooks, lists, end cards with the URL. Vishnu loves it (2026-10-23).
- **Avoid:** Stickers covering each other's words; more than seven on screen.
