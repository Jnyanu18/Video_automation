# Style Guide: High-Energy Event Aftermovies

Use this style profile whenever editing an event promo, aftermovie, or conference highlight reel.

## 1. Visual Framing & Layout
- **Aspect Ratio:** Vertical 9:16 (1080 × 1920).
- **Framing:** Dynamic center crop (`crop=ih*9/16:ih:(iw-ow)/2:0,scale=1080:1920:flags=lanczos`).
- **Pacing:** Fast, dynamic cuts synchronized with music beats (shot durations: 1.0s – 3.2s).
- **Color Grading:** Commercial high contrast (`eq=contrast=1.16:brightness=0.02:saturation=1.22`), deep rich blacks, vibrant cyan and gold lighting highlights.

## 2. Motion Graphics & Typography (HyperFrames)
- **Palette:** 
  - Primary Accent: Luxury Gold (`#D4AF37`) & Bright Gold (`#FFE885`)
  - Tech Accent: Electric Cyan (`#00E5FF`)
  - Base: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) on Dark Frosted Glass (`rgba(10, 15, 25, 0.75)`)
- **Typography:** Heavy modern sans-serif (`Montserrat` / `Inter` Black 900 caps).
- **Card Elements:**
  - Glassmorphism backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`), gold border radius (`24px`).
  - Drop Cards: High-impact central kinetic reveals with scale & blur transitions.
  - Lower Thirds: Left-aligned badge with gold indicator strip for VIPs / speakers.
  - Finale: Luxury gold-bordered badge with event organizer credit.

## 3. Audio & Sound Design
- **Music:** 128 BPM cinematic hybrid electronic / trailer EDM with punchy kicks, 808 sub drops, and riser build-ups.
- **Sound FX:**
  - Sub-bass boom on visual hook (`0:00`), bass drop (`0:10`), and final resolution.
  - Snare build-up / riser rolls before energy drops.
  - Subtle camera shutter clicks and whoosh transitions across cuts.
  - Event ambient sound ducked at -16dB under the master soundtrack.
