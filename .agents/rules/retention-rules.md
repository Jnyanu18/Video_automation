# Viral Retention & Directing Rules (Adapted from Vishnu's Studio)

Apply these rules across all client video production tasks in this workspace:

## 1. The First Two Seconds (Hook & Velocity)
- **First spoken word:** Within `0.3s` of frame 0 (trim any lead-in dead air).
- **First visual shift:** Something visibly changes by `1.0s` (a punch-in zoom, card flip, or headline pop).
- **Movement tempo:** In the first 5 seconds, no shot or graphic holds static for longer than `1.5s`.

## 2. The Storyboard Approval Gate
- **Never do a heavy render before the user approves the storyboard.**
- Present a clear breakdown (timestamp, what is spoken, visual composition, sound design, motion) and wait for the user's approval.
- Render low-resolution previews first before full 1080x1920 delivery renders.

## 3. Direction: "A New Picture on Every Phrase"
- Plan one visual state per spoken phrase (about one per second).
- When the speaker says a noun, show the object, card, or interface.
- Never leave a headline sitting alone on a blank screen while the voice speaks.
- Mocks and diagrams must hold believable, realistic data — never an empty placeholder.

## 4. The "Purple Cow" Test
- Every video must have **one remarkable, true thing** in the first 2 seconds that makes it impossible to scroll past.
- It must pass the **Remark Test** (would a viewer tell a friend about this?) and the **Truth Test** (is it real, checkable, and paid off before the end?).

## 5. Audio & Foley Standards
- Use the built-in CC0 sound library in `assets/sfx/` (`whoosh`, `subdrop`, `riser`, `pop`, `ding`, `glitch`, `cash`).
- Background music ducked between `-19 LUFS` and `-22 LUFS` to ensure voice clarity.
- Soft SFX on UI clicks and pops; deep sub-bass impact only on major drops and hook moments.
