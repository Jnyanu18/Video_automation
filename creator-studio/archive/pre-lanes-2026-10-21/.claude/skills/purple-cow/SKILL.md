# Purple cow

From Seth Godin: a brown cow is invisible, a purple cow is something people remark on. In a feed, the
viewer decides in about one second. The purple cow is the one thing in this video they have not seen
today, shown in the first frame and said in the first words.

## The two tests (both must pass)

1. **Remark test:** would a viewer describe this video to a friend in one sentence *because of this
   thing*? ("A guy read out the bot message that got him called out.")
2. **Truth test:** is it real, checkable, and does the video pay it off? If the payoff does not match
   the promise, it is bait, and bait costs the brand more than a skipped video.

## Where a true cow comes from (Vishnu's own material)

Look here first, in this order. Never invent one.

- **An uncomfortable real number.** 26 people signed up and none of them used it. 152 invites, 0
  replies. Numbers come from `docs/53-OUTREACH-LOG.md` in the Verb repo, the admin, or the brain.
- **A confession.** Missed a booked call to build a feature nobody asked for.
- **Doing the thing on camera.** Asking the agent to do a real multi-step task live, with the approval
  card. Reading the actual message that got him called out.
- **A real object or screen nobody shows.** The inbox with 8 pitches and 0 customers. The cancel-plan
  screen of a tool.
- **A true contrarian line.** "I cancelled the tool that was supposed to get me customers."
- **An unexpected format for a normal topic.** A receipt, a court case, a countdown, a before and after,
  a split screen of the long way and the short way.

## Rules

- One cow per video. Two cows are a brown cow.
- It shows in the first frame *and* is heard in the first sentence. No logo, no "hey guys", no
  "in today's video", no "did you know".
- The video pays it off before the end. Note the time of the payoff in the brief.
- No shock for its own sake, no fake numbers, no one else's private details, no client without written
  permission, no em dashes, no hype words ("game changer", "revolutionary").
- Prefer a real shot over a generated one. AI-looking visuals read as slop.
- On-screen text is two to four words or one number, never a full sentence. The first video's hook
  ("6 apps this year. 1 makes money." in a panel) read as templated; numbers on their own did not.

## How to use it at the brief stage

Offer Vishnu three candidate cows from the list above, each as one line, then he picks. Write the pick
into `brief.md`:

```
Purple cow: <one sentence>
Shows at: <first frame, and the first words>
Pays off at: <roughly when, and how>
```

## Checklist before the script is approved

- [ ] The first two seconds contain the cow, visually and in words.
- [ ] Both tests pass.
- [ ] The number or claim has a source written in the brief.
- [ ] The payoff exists in the script.
- [ ] If `story-loops` is used too, the cow opens loop A.
