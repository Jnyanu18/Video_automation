## Focus

- **gap**: pros vs you, a primary source, three steps. 35 anthropic-gap 40.9K, the account's best by 2x. Only 1 reel: test it next.
- **free-x**: your own X for $0, replaces something paid. 16 build-your-own-x 20.5K, 06 free-business-email 13.6K,
  08 saas-for-zero 11.3K, 11 aws-credits 3.1K, 13 build-your-jarvis-v2 3.7K. "Your own" in the hook is the best phrase on the account.
- **free-tool**: a free tool for *your* problem, "you" in line one. 32 floorplan-map 12.6K, 38 freellmapi 11.6K,
  25 lemo-opuscar 4.1K.

## Less

- **list**: works only broad and free (15 thirty-claude-plugins 6.5K); niche or abstract lists flop (26 security-top-10
  ~1.4K, 31 rocket-styles ~1.3K, 34 three-agents 1.3K).
- **fear**: 1.3K to 2.5K (17 backdoor, 18 binance, 19 black-october, 04 security-week 1.3K, 27 fridges 1.4K).

## Stop

- **product** (AskVerb reels): 104 to 2.8K, mostly under 900 (20, 21, 22, 02).
- **founder** (pitches, journey): 188 to 1.5K (03, 05, 01). Exception: the YC founder video 5.4K, because it is about
  how *you* get into YC.
- **news / research**: 1.4K to 1.9K (28 weights, 40 ai-sleep, 37 mouse surgery, 10 yc-pitch-data 253).
- **third-party**: someone else's achievement as the hook. 44 ecc-dev-team 1.5K, 41 reverse-anything 1.6K, 24 harvey 509.
- **sequel**: 36 saas-for-zero-2 1.4K after part 1's 11.3K.

## What did not predict views

- Talking head vs animated: talking heads range 188 to 5.4K.
