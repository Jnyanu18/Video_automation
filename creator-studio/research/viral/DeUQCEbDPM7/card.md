## Second look (2026-11-07, at 12 frames a second, after Vishnu rejected agency-we-got-you)

What the first notes missed, and what agency-we-got-you got wrong:

- **Only the text changes.** The green background, the logo and the tagline never move or change. The question is dark
  text, the answer is white text, on the same background. (I split the whole frame into two worlds, so a hard seam cut
  through the logo and the footer on every swing.)
- **The string is the cut line.** The pivot is far above the frame (about 0.8 of the frame's height above the top), so the
  string is long and nearly vertical: about 9 degrees each side, the badge travelling from 0.11 to 0.88 of the width. The
  text is cut exactly where the string crosses it. (Mine: a short string at 27 degrees, and a vertical cut at the badge, so
  the cut sat about 100 px away from the string and looked detached.)
- **Timing:** a plain pendulum, about 1.18 s a swing, 2.4 s a question. Sound hits in pairs: one where the swing turns, one
  about 0.5 s later as the string slices the text (1.8 hits a second).
- **It loops.** 12 s, five questions (branding, a website, graphics, to grow, SEO help), and the last frame lands back on
  "NEED BRANDING?", the first frame. Instagram replays it without a seam. No end card, no call to action in the motion;
  the URL sits at the bottom the whole time. (I added a damped stop and an end card, which broke the loop.)
- **One text shape, one size.** Every question is "NEED / <two or three words>?", 2 lines, the same size. (Mine changed
  the wording and the size per question.)
- **Depth:** the string and badge cast a soft shadow on the background (a faint second line beside the string).
- **Sound is wide stereo** (side 5 dB above mid, L/R correlation -0.52): it moves left and right with the badge. Mine was
  near mono. Ref: -16.4 LUFS, 89 BPM pulse, no voice.
