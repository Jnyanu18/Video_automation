# Memphis

1980s Memphis Group: loud flat colours, squiggles, triangles, dots and zigzags.

Reference tiles: `memphis` in `remotion/src/lanes/styles/StyleSheet.tsx` (a headline, a chat and a stat). Build from them.

- **Colours:** Cream `#FFF1D0`, pink `#FF5C8A`, teal `#00C2D1`, yellow `#FFD23F`, purple `#5B2EFF`, ink `#1a1a1a`.
- **Type:** Righteous.
- **Shapes and texture:** 4 to 6px black outlines, hard offset shadows in a second colour (`10px 10px 0 #5B2EFF`), scattered shapes in the corners (squiggle, triangle, ring, dot grid, zigzag), small tilts.
- **Motion:** Shapes wiggle and drift a few pixels; labels stamp in.
- **Use for:** Playful hooks, lists, a fun stat.
- **Avoid:** Shapes behind text; more than five shapes on screen.
