Imagine your app launches at nine tomorrow morning, and it's 11:58 at night.
You're completely alone.

The developer who built it stopped replying three hours ago.
Phone off. No messages.

You run one last test payment with your own card.
Declined.

You try again, slower this time, and the screen says the same thing.
Payment failed.

A warning flashes in the corner: three percent battery, and the charger is at the office.
Of course it is.

So you open the code to find the problem, and at the top of the main file there's a note: rule one, never deploy at midnight.
The rest is deleted.

You smile, because it's probably a joke between developers.
Just a joke.

[whispers] Then the clock in the corner turns from 11:59 to midnight,
and the screen goes black.

The battery is dead. In the silence, your phone lights up on the desk.
One notification.

"Your app has been approved." The launch is on, and two hundred people are waiting for it.
The payments still don't work.

You have two choices. Launch at nine with broken payments, or delay it and tell everyone why.
Nine hours left.

[curious] So, what would you do?
Comment one or two.

Or skip that night completely, with a team that builds it with you and stays after launch.
Build with Auralion Labs.
