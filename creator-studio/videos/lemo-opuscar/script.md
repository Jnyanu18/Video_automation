It's called Lemo-Opuscar, it's MIT licensed, and it's picked up over eleven thousand stars in about five weeks.

Pick a style like crayon picture book, watercolor or ink wash, then tell it your story. Claude directs the rest.

You install it with two commands, and it's tuned for Claude Opus 5.5.
