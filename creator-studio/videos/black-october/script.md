Black October isn't over yet. One day to go.

Earlier this month, PHP rushed out fixes for two critical flaws hackers were already using. Node.js patched ten of its own.

Microsoft fixed over a thousand bugs in one Patch Tuesday. Cisco's firewalls got hit too.

That was just the warm-up.

In the last two days, four big hacks landed. None used the front door.

Binance lost billions from one hot wallet. It started with a laptop.

Polymarket users lost about 2.7 million dollars in an hour. A hacked outside provider let it in.

Hackers posted the details of over 40,000 people working for DHS. They claim a login service got them in.

And a poisoned Claude Code update went out in Cursor's marketplace. Up to 5,000 installs.

Everyone's asking if AI did this. Nobody has shown that yet.

Every one came through something trusted. Check yours.
