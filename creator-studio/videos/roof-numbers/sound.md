## Music sections

| Section | Beats | About | What the music does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The storm | storm, strange, laugh | 0 to 10 s | Curious and serious, like a folk tale or a nature documentary. Muted plucks, soft ticking, no bass, no drums. A playful pluck on "lost it". |
| The morning | morning, scan | 10 to 18 s | Warmer, a light pulse joins as the phones go up. Still no bass. |
| Every phone rings | ring, ladder | 18 to 23 s | Sub-bass comes in under everything, tension climbs. The same loudness, just heavier. |
| Answered | answered | 23 to 28 s | The tension releases into a bright figure; light drums enter. |
| The reveal | reveal, brand | 28 to 34 s | One beat of near silence on "Because the voice", then the busiest part: full drums, quick hits. |
| Ava and the end | does, end | 34 to 45 s | Steady and warm, fewer hits under the voice, then one clean final chord and a short tail. |

Keep the loudness level, as the reference does. The changes are bass in, rhythm up, then thinning out.
No volume swells.

## Sound effects

- **storm:** wind dying away, a dripping gutter.
- **strange:** a sticker pressed down, four times, a soft slap each.
- **laugh:** a short dog whine.
- **morning:** birds, doors opening one after another.
- **scan:** the phone camera's lock-on beep, then more beeps, quicker, as phones go up along the block.
- **ring:** one phone buzz, then more layered in until it's a crowd of ringing.
- **ladder:** the buzzing keeps going under him; shingles knocked into place.
- **answered:** the ringing cuts to a pick-up click, then a soft pop per "Booked" tag.
- **reveal:** under the narrator, quietly: Ava's real greeting ("Hi, thanks for calling...") from the
  Lanternfield demo agent, cut to two or three words.
- **does:** a soft tick as each pill lands.
- **end:** a soft chime on "Ava".

## Narrator voice

A calm, low documentary voice, serious through act 1 so the tall tale lands, a touch warmer from "It
rings Ava". Pick by ear with `speak.py --sample`.
