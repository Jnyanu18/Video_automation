# Brief: Google's 12 tester rule

<!-- Stage 0. Written with Claude in conversation before a word of script. -->

- **Brand:** personal (Vishnu), narrated in his cloned voice (ElevenLabs v3).
- **Who it is for:** people building their first Android app; the audience that saved the Play Store carousel
  and DMs him about publishing.
- **The one point:** a new personal Play account cannot go live until 12 testers keep the app for 14 days, and
  internal testing does not count; plan for it before you finish building.
- **The hook (first 2 seconds):** "Google won't let you publish your app until 12 people keep it on their phones
  for 14 days." (purple cow: a surprising, true rule most first-timers learn too late.)
- **Loops (story-loops):** A opens at the hook (how do you get through it?) and closes at the end (line up testers
  early, or an organisation account). B opens and closes in the middle (internal testing looks like the way in,
  and is not).
- **Shape and length:** 9:16, about 40 seconds, seven paragraphs, every frame drawn in code.
- **The visual idea:** a dark Play Console look in Android green. Twelve phones light up one by one; a 14-day
  calendar ticks; the Internal track gets struck out and Closed lights up; a dashboard counter fills; a "Apply
  for production" button with a review clock; two routes at the end (testers first, or an organisation badge).
  Theme `console-green`.
- **Real numbers, and where they come from:** 12 testers, 14 days in a row, accounts made after 13 Nov 2023, review
  usually 7 days or less (Google Play Console Help 14151465); organisation accounts with a D-U-N-S number are not
  in scope (same page, personal accounts only); two apps published (Vakya, GT-FIVE).
- **The question it ends on:** none; it ends on "save this, follow for the next step".
