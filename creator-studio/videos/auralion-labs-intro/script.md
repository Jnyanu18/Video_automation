A plumber is under a sink. His phone rings. He can't answer, so that customer calls someone else.

We're Auralion Labs, a product studio. And right now we're building something for exactly that problem: a website and an AI receptionist for local service businesses.

The receptionist answers every call, books the job straight into the owner's calendar, or takes a message and sends it on. The website is built from the business's own Google Maps listing.

Our own studio already runs on it. Our receptionist, Ava, answers our phone and books our calls.

We're building this in public, and I'll show what works and what doesn't, as we go.

If you run a service business, or you build for one, follow along.
