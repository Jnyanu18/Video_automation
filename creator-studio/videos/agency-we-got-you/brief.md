## Direction

One look, the website's: a light page (`#F5F5F7`, ink `#161722`, the dotted stage) for the question, an ink page with the
same dotted stage for the answer, Poppins 800 (the site's font), no colour accent at all. The badge is ink with a white
ring so it shows on both sides of the wipe. Real asset: our own mark (`auralionlabs.svg`), on the badge and in the logo at
the top. No mascot, no mock interface: this reel is type and motion.

The hook moment is frame 0: the badge is already swinging at the far right with the first question on screen.
No word is spoken, so the "first word within 0.3 s" rule has nothing to trim; the picture changes inside 1 s.

| Time | On screen | What moves | Sound |

The swing never changes speed (a swing is 1.2 s, so a question and its answer take 2.4 s): the sameness is the
hook, as in the reference. Nothing is still for more than about 1.2 s.
