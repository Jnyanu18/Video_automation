Email on your own domain usually costs a few dollars per person, every month. This free tool takes it to zero.

It's called OpenInbox. It runs in your own Cloudflare account on the free plan: click Deploy, paste one token, and it sets up your domain's mail records for you. Add as many mailboxes as you want, with no per-seat cost.

It's in beta, so try it on a spare domain first.

## X

OpenInbox: free business email on your own domain, running in your own Cloudflare account on the free plan.

Deploy, paste one token, and it writes your mail records. Unlimited mailboxes, no per-seat fees. Sends through Resend or Brevo, and falls back to the next when one hits its free limit.
