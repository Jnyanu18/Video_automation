- **The one point:** setup steps were not the blocker; touching your live product and having no urgent reason were.
- **Purple cow:** not used.
- **Loops:** (skill: story-loops) A+ "I cut setup from ten steps to three" / A- "still zero", closed when a stranger on Reddit explains why.
- **Shape and length:** stuck point, what I tried, what happened, the lesson, one question. About 25 seconds; the old 45-second cut lost the story (Vishnu, 2026-10-23). Rhythm: every long line is followed by a short one of five words or fewer (Vishnu, 2026-10-23).
- **Real numbers, and where each comes from:** 23 sign-ups and 0 live on their own product (the Day 22 recap, docs/53); setup cut from 10 steps to 3 (the quick start, shipped before Day 22).
