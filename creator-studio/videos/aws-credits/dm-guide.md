How to get $1,000 in AWS credits (AWS Activate Founders)

AWS Activate Founders gives self-funded startups $1,000 in AWS credits. I got mine: $1,000, valid for two years.

Before you apply, check you have these:
1. A real company website on your own domain (not a free-host link like yourapp.vercel.app or a site builder subdomain). It should say what your product does.
2. A business email on that same domain, like you@yourcompany.com. You can set one up free with Zoho Mail; my reel shows how: https://www.instagram.com/reel/De-Bv73SnpX/
3. A startup that is self-funded (no institutional investors), founded in the last 10 years, and has never received AWS Activate credits before.
4. An AWS account on the paid tier. Add a payment card in the AWS console. The credits cover your usage first; anything beyond the credits is billed, so set a budget alert.

How to apply:
1. Sign in to your AWS account, or create one at https://aws.amazon.com and add your card.
2. Go to https://aws.amazon.com/activate/credits and choose the Founders package.
3. Fill in your company details: name, website, founding date, and your business email.
4. Enter your AWS account ID (top right of the AWS console, under your account name).
5. Submit and wait for the approval email. When it's approved, the credits appear in the AWS console under Billing, then Credits.

After approval:
1. Set a budget alert: in the console go to Billing, then Budgets, and create a monthly budget so you're emailed before you spend past your credits.
2. Check the expiry date on the Credits page. Mine runs for two years.
3. While you're there, AWS also gives small extra credits for trying things like setting up a budget or launching an EC2 instance. I got two of $20 each, plus $100 with the Free Tier.

Terms change, so check the Activate page before you apply.
