# Lessons (taste principles learned from client feedback)

Read this before planning. Each lesson is a principle with the reason behind it, never "make it like video X". Add a line after every approval or rejection.

| # | Principle | Why (the feedback behind it) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Never fill a frame with a small panel or UI floating in empty background. Fill the frame with full-bleed colour, display type or a hero object. | A template-built ad with small centred panels on dark space was rejected as generic and poor. |
| 2 | Sound must be a full, beat-locked score at −14 LUFS. A few quiet sound effects are not sound design. | An SFX-only mix peaking at −20 dB made the same picture feel cheap. |
| 3 | Fast, beat-locked cutting with full-bleed colour flips and kinetic type is the house taste when energy is asked for. | The approved work had about 16–20 cuts in 15 s, half-beat word bursts and colour flips. |
| 4 | Never ask clients for reference videos. Derive the direction from the brief and the brand's meaning. | Product requirement: clients give prompts, not references. |
| 5 | A conflicting brief (a calm brand asking for fast beats) becomes a contrast arc: the fast part is the problem state, the calm part is the resolution. | It turned a contradiction into the concept. |
| 6 | Music must be a professionally produced, licensed track (e.g. Mixkit, free for commercial use), edited only on its own bar lines, with recorded effects and the client's own location sound on top. Never ship a score synthesized from sine waves and noise. | NAATI FARMS v1 used a code-synthesized score with "boing" springs and noise hits: "the sound is very bad, like very". |
| 7 | Write the script from the client's facts only: what they sell, where, their claims, how to buy. Never invent rituals, places or lessons the client did not state, and flag every line of your own copy for confirmation. | NAATI FARMS v1 invented a dawn-ride/gate story and a Kannada lesson, and never said the farm sells chicken: "the script is very off". |
| 8 | When the client supplies real footage, keep the footage dominant. Do not cover it with large flat brand-colour cards, and keep the palette restrained (ask when no brand kit exists). | NAATI FARMS v1 alternated big green, yolk and cream cards with the footage: "the colour combination is also bad". The owner then chose black & white + one accent. |
