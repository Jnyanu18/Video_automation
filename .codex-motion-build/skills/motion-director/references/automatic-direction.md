# Automatic direction from sparse or mixed prompts

Turn the prompt into a clear film before choosing effects. Use the saved brand, approved assets, resolved brief and renderer capabilities with the compact design catalog. A style name is a proposed treatment, not proof that a component exists.

## Evaluate the whole compact catalog

Consider every available card before selecting a direction. Compare fit to the objective, audience, subject action, available assets, brand character, text density, duration and execution support. Do not stop at the first matching keyword or give an elaborate effect priority merely because it is available. Retrieve detailed recipes and atlas entries for the selected cards after this compact comparison.

Choose one primary family to govern layout, contrast, spatial density and camera grammar. Add up to two secondary families, each with a named job and a limited moment: opening typography, image selection, readable feedback or a closing accent. Zero secondary families is valid. Record the reason for each choice and what it contributes. A mixed prompt should produce one coordinated journey; remove effects that compete with its focal action or shorten essential reading time.

## Infer reversibly and preserve identity

Resolve the subject and desired viewer action from the prompt and trusted brand context. Saved kit values and prior project decisions supply defaults; explicit prompt instructions and explicitly touched form controls override ordinary defaults under the [brand/brief contract](brand-and-brief.md). Respect configured locks and report conflicts. Mood words can change staging and rhythm without silently changing fonts, colors or identity.

For a one-line or abstract prompt, propose reversible choices for motion energy, composition, wording and transitions. State material assumptions in the editable preview plan. Use the host's duration, format and language defaults when unspecified. Do not turn an inference into quoted prompt evidence, an approved fact or a permanent brand preference.

Ask one focused question when the real subject, required asset or factual message is genuinely missing and cannot be recovered from the kit. Cosmetic uncertainty can proceed as an editable draft. Generic claims such as “saves hours,” “trusted by thousands” or “award-winning” still require evidence; a sparse prompt never authorizes them. Never invent clients, metrics, prices, a booking destination or an approved voice.

On revisions, preserve unrelated scene IDs, assets, languages, copy and direction. Reconsider the relevant selection when the user changes it. Apply a newer kit through explicit rebase rather than silently restyling an existing film.

## Plan backward, then choreograph the body

Reserve the ending's approved identity, CTA and readable hold first. Work backward through the payoff, concrete body action and opening promise. At the chosen FPS, derive integer scene boundaries from one timing source. Give each beat a subject action, focal element, text role and explicit handover; use a cut when the thought changes. Stage anticipation, travel, contact and settlement, then check the last revealed line's reading hold.

Prefer the actual recording's measured beats and voice alignment. A BPM number does not establish beat phase, and estimated narration is provisional. Preserve comprehension when music and speech compete. Do not label inferred timing as measured beat synchronization.

After choosing a direction, execute [reusable-execution.md](reusable-execution.md): persist the shot decisions, choose an implemented component or custom build, render and inspect it, and repair observed failures. A sparse prompt may imply a custom mechanism even when the user does not name it; standard/custom selection follows the chosen action and actual capabilities. A selected catalog ID is not evidence that the basic registry can render it.

## Illustrative combinations

- **“Make our photo studio feel bold.”** R02 can carry one portfolio image through connected states; R03 supplies the opening type accent; R05 supplies a brief selection moment. This supports a coherent image-led journey. Use approved photos; persistent geometry needs the adapted custom composition demonstrated by [FRAME / STUDIO](../examples/frame-photo-studio/README.md).
- **“A calm AI launch: paste a link, see the output, understand the benefit.”** R08 leads the UI hierarchy, R06 supplies input-to-result causality, and R01 contributes a restrained payoff. Use approved proof or show a real product action; do not invent a growth metric.
- **“A warm, patient plant-care lesson in French.”** R11 leads illustration and narration; R08 can add one useful checklist. Longer explanatory holds matter more than another visual family. Preserve the supplied language and supported factual script.

These are directing examples, not measured model-evaluation results. Balance creative fit with execution: distinguish implemented templates, source recipes and unsupported effects. Use the custom-renderer handoff when required; do not silently replace a requested action with generic panels. Catalog and schema checks establish structure, while adapted rendering, listening and playback review establish what the film actually does.

## Extend the catalog

Add a card with a new ID such as `R12` in [design-catalog.json](design-catalog.json). Summarize the purpose, motion, pace and constraints. Point `detail` to a local Markdown file in `references/` or to a heading like `references/reference-atlas.md#r12` with the heading `## R12 — Title`. Use stable IDs so previous projects can still resolve their references. The adapter and runtime schemas load the catalog directly. Run `npm run schema` in `starter/` to refresh the exported schemas for external consumers.
