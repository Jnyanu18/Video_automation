# Halftone / Pop art

Comic-book pop art: Ben-Day dots, speech bubbles, starbursts, thick black lines.

Reference tiles: `popart` in `remotion/src/lanes/styles/StyleSheet.tsx` (a headline, a chat and a stat). Build from them.

- **Colours:** Yellow `#FFE600`, red `#FF3B3B`, cyan `#00B7EB`, ink `#111`, white.
- **Type:** Bungee.
- **Shapes and texture:** Dot grounds (`radial-gradient` dots, 22 to 26px), 8px black outlines, speech bubbles with round corners, a starburst clip-path (`burst()`) for a shout, photos turned into red halftone (grayscale multiplied on red, yellow dots screened over).
- **Motion:** Bursts punch in with overshoot; a quick shake on impact.
- **Use for:** Shock lines, short facts over his photo, reactions.
- **Avoid:** Long text; calm or serious points.
