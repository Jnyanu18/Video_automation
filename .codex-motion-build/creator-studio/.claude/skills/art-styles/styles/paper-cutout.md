# Paper cutout / Collage

Torn paper, cut-out words like a ransom note, masking tape, on kraft paper.

Reference tiles: `collage` in `remotion/src/lanes/styles/StyleSheet.tsx` (a headline, a chat and a stat). Build from them.

- **Colours:** Kraft `#C8A27A`, off-white paper `#fffaf0`, black `#1b1b1b`, red `#E84A2E`, blue `#2E6FE8`, yellow `#F2C94C`, tape `rgba(255,244,200,0.75)`.
- **Type:** Playfair Display 900 for the paper, Permanent Marker for the stuck-on words.
- **Shapes and texture:** Torn edges with the `torn()` clip-path, a drop shadow under each piece, tape strips across corners, every piece tilted 2 to 4 degrees, photos cut out and taped down, grain at 0.3.
- **Motion:** Pieces slap down one at a time with a tiny rotate; stop-motion feel (hold each pose 2 to 3 frames).
- **Use for:** Covers, carousels, story moments, his photo. Vishnu loved it (2026-10-23).
- **Avoid:** Perfect alignment (it kills the look); tape over the words.
