Here's how to put an AI agent inside your product that does things for your users. It takes three steps.

Step one, go to askverb.com and add your domain, and it builds a read-only agent from your public site. No code yet.

Step two, paste one script tag, and that agent is live inside your product. Users ask, it answers.

Step three, connect the Verb MCP, and your coding agent writes the verb.md and the tools that let it act. Now it acts in their account.

In most cases you're live in under an hour. Completely free to start.
