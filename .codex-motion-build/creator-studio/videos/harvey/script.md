This Claude agent has only one job, and that is to get you customers. You tell it what you sell and who your dream customer is, and it goes hunting.

Say you sell online booking to dentists. It finds local practices, reads their websites, and picks the ones with no booking page.

Then it writes each one an email about something real it found on their site. Every email is different.

It reads the replies and drafts your answer, and nothing sends until you approve it.

The craziest part is it runs on the Claude plan you already pay for, with no API bill. A dashboard shows it all.
