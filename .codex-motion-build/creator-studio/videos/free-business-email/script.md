Your own business email, for zero dollars. My Reddit post on this hit twenty thousand views.

you at yourbrand dot com tells a client you're real. A Gmail address makes them wonder.

Zoho gives you five free addresses. Finding them is the hard part.

I signed up on Zoho Mail, and the free plan was hidden. The paid trial was right there instead.

The free plan lives on a different page: Zoho Workplace. Sign up there, and it shows up.

Pick the free plan. Verify your domain with one TXT record. Add the MX records, so mail arrives. Add SPF and DKIM, so you skip spam. Then create up to five addresses.

Two things to know. You need a domain first, about ten dollars a year. And the free plan runs in the browser and Zoho's app, in select regions like India and Europe.
