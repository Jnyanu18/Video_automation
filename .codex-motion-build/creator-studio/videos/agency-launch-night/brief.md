# Brief: Launch night

- **Brand:** agency (Auralion Labs). Final to `agency/videos/`.
- **Funnel:** top.
- **Who it is for:** founders building their first app, especially those working with one freelancer or alone.
- **The one point:** launch night alone is a nightmare; a team that builds with you and stays after launch is the way
  out. Shown, never said, until the last line.
- **Reference:** DfIjwMcN-XQ (the last train home, 99 s, about 20 cuts). Study note: `research/viral/DfIjwMcN-XQ/card.md`.
- **What we borrow:** "Imagine you", problems stacked one per shot with a prop in close-up (the clock, the declined
  card, the battery warning), a found rule with the rest missing, a countdown to midnight, the lights going out, a face
  close-up per emotion, two labelled choices (1 LAUNCH / 2 DELAY) and "what would you do?".
- **Our twist:** the nightmare is one our buyers really have; the threat is a broken launch, not a ghost; the brand comes in
  only after the choice.
- **Story logic, checked:** you are alone because the developer stopped replying; the screen goes black because the battery
  was at 3% and the charger is elsewhere; you laugh at the note because it reads like a joke; the approval arrives at
  midnight, which makes the broken payments urgent; the choice follows from both.
- **Loops:** A+ "Imagine your app launches..." / B+ the note's missing rules (never answered: the note is a mood, like the
  reference) / C+ "the screen goes black", closed at C- "One notification" / A- "what would you do?".
- **Shape and length:** 9:16, about 80 s at Christopher's pace (222 words; first estimated at 60 to 65 s, which was
  wrong), a new shot every 3 to 5 s.
- **Facts:** none about the real world. Two hundred people and 3% are story details. The last line ("builds your app with
  you and stays after launch") matches the site's "We build relationships, not one-off projects."
- **Comment prompt:** "Comment one or two" (the engagement); no keyword or guide (a story reel, like the reference).
- **Decided (Vishnu, 2026-11-09):** 3D in Remotion with the studio's 3D tools; Christopher with emotion cues; music from
  Openverse only. **Open:** voice credits (about 490 needed, 337 left on key 2, reset 2026-11-22; key 1 rejected).
