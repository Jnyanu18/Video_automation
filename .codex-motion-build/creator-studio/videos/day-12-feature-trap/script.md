# I hid in the product instead of selling

Day twelve of trying to get ten paying customers. And I did the worst thing a founder can do.

I spent the whole day building a new feature. Team invites and account sharing. Useful, one day. But nobody is using the product yet.

And I was so deep in it that I missed a call someone had booked with me.

Meanwhile, the one thing I had automated kept going without me. One hundred and forty founders invited on LinkedIn. Nine connected. Zero replies. Zero paying customers.

So here is the lesson. If nobody uses what you have already built, another feature will not fix it. Distribution is the job.

Tomorrow, no features. Just outreach. What do you do to stop hiding in the product when selling gets uncomfortable?
