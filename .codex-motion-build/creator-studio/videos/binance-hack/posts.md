## Instagram

The largest hack in history took three minutes. Here's what we actually know.

What happened: on October 29 at 20:20 UTC, one Ethereum transaction carrying 50 signed authorizations hit a Binance hot wallet, the one that pays out withdrawals. One of them matched. By about 20:24 the wallet had sent roughly $2.5 billion to the attacker.

How it started: Binance found no leaked private key. Its first finding points to one DevOps engineer's compromised laptop.

How big: estimates for the whole incident run from about $2.8 billion to $6.3 billion. Even the lowest is well past Bybit's $1.5 billion record from 2025.

The twist: Tether froze more than 1.5 billion USDT and Circle more than 800 million USDC, right inside the attacker's wallets. The ETH can't be frozen.

What's still unconfirmed: the final number, and who did it.

Sources: Blockaid, Binance, Tether's Paolo Ardoino, Circle's Jeremy Allaire, via crypto.news and Cryptobriefing.
