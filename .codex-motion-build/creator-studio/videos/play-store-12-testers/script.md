[serious] Google won't let you publish your app until twelve people keep it on their phones for fourteen days.

[matter-of-fact] I've published two apps on Google Play, and this is the rule nobody warns you about.

Twelve testers have to opt in and keep the app installed for fourteen days in a row. You can watch the count on your Play Console dashboard.

[sighs] Only then can you apply for production, and Google can take up to a week to review it.

[curious] So how do you get through it? [confident] Line up your twelve testers before you finish building, not after. Or register as an organisation with a DUNS number, and the rule doesn't apply.
