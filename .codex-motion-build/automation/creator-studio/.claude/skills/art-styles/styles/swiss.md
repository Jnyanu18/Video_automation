# Swiss / International Typographic

Strict grid, huge sans type set flush left, one red, lots of white.

Reference tiles: `swiss` in `remotion/src/lanes/styles/StyleSheet.tsx` (a headline, a chat and a stat). Build from them.

- **Colours:** White `#fff`, black `#111`, one red `#E30613`.
- **Type:** Helvetica Neue / Arial bold, tight tracking (-2 to -4px), small caps labels at 20px.
- **Shapes and texture:** Thick black rules (4 to 8px), a red square or bar as the only shape, no radius anywhere, numbers enormous and cropped.
- **Motion:** Hard cuts and wipes along the grid lines; nothing bounces.
- **Use for:** Sometimes only (Vishnu, 2026-10-23): serious points, a big number, a quote.
- **Avoid:** Soft shadows, rounded corners, a second colour.
