# Kinetic typography

The words are the picture: huge condensed type that fills the frame and moves with the voice.

Reference tiles: `kinetic` in `remotion/src/lanes/styles/StyleSheet.tsx` (a headline, a chat and a stat). Build from them.

- **Colours:** Black `#0b0b0b`, white, one yellow `#FFE14D`.
- **Type:** Anton, uppercase, sizes from 90 to 600px on one screen.
- **Shapes and texture:** Lines of different sizes stacked tight (line height 0.86), one word outlined (`WebkitTextStroke`), words cropped by the frame edge, a vertical word down the side.
- **Motion:** Every word lands on its spoken syllable: slam in, scale punch, slide, swap. This is the one style that only works in motion.
- **Use for:** Hooks, the one sentence a video is about, voice-over without a face.
- **Avoid:** More than one accent colour; small text.
