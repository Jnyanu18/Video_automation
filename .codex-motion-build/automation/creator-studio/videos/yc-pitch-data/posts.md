## Instagram

I went through 188 real YC founder videos where we know the outcome. 118 got in, 70 didn't.

The founders' backgrounds didn't separate them. Big-company jobs, PhDs, past startups: the rejected founders mentioned theirs just as often.

What did separate them was money. 44% of accepted founders talked about revenue or paying customers. Only 16% of rejected ones did.

4 in 5 accepted videos had at least one real number in them.

Length made no difference. Both groups landed around one minute, so use your minute on the number that matters.
