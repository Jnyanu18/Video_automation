Hackers reportedly turned supermarket fridges up to fifty degrees, and how they got in is almost boring.

Stores threw the food out.

<!-- B+ -->
It began in France in October, when attackers took over the fridges in hundreds of supermarkets. Intermarché's owner put the losses at several million euros.

But on Monday it happened again, in seven countries.

<!-- A- -->
Both times, the way in was fridge software from a Danish company called Danfoss, and it says the stores hit hadn't updated it.

<!-- B- C+ -->
But the fridges were not the only thing that broke that weekend.

Germany's cyber agency told lawmakers to treat their iPhones as possibly hacked.

Apple had warned them about spyware.

Then late Sunday, the emergency alert apps in both Ukraine and Russia were hacked to send fake nuclear warnings.

Russia's reached thirty-three regions.

<!-- C- -->
Nobody has claimed the fridge attacks, and nobody has shown that any of this is connected.
