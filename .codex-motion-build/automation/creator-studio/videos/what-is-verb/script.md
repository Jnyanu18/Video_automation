You know that feeling when you're using an app, and you know it can do the thing you want, but you just can't find where?

That's why I built Verb.

You add it to your product, and your users can just type what they want. Something like, "move my Friday meeting to Monday." And Verb actually does it, right there in your app, as that user. It can't do anything they couldn't already do themselves.

And if it's about to change something, it asks first. The user sees exactly what's going to happen, and says yes or no.

Setting it up is mostly done by your coding agent. It reads your app, writes down what Verb is allowed to do, and you paste one line of code.

It's free right now while I launch it, and it's already running in production for a real company.

So I'm curious. If your users could just ask your product for one thing, what would it be?
