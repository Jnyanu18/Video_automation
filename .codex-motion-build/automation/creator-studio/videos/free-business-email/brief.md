# Brief: A business email for zero dollars

- **Brand:** personal (Vishnu). The first video of the rebuilt animated lane (`lanes/animated/PLAN.md`); its scenes
  become the first entries of the scene library.
- **Who it is for:** early founders and freelancers who own a domain and still email clients from Gmail.
- **The one point:** five free addresses on your own domain exist, behind one sign-up page most people miss.
- **The hook (first 5 seconds):** "Your own business email, for zero dollars." A gold $0 stamps onto an envelope on
  the first word; the 20K view counter by 2 s.
- **Shape and length:** hook and proof, why it matters, the hidden door (the loop opens at about 0:05 and closes at
  about 0:16), five fast steps, two things to know, the ask. About 40 s, 9:16.
- **The metaphor or visual idea:** a locked door. The five free addresses sit behind it; Workplace is the key.
- **Real numbers it uses, and where they come from:** 20,000 views (Vishnu, r/SaaS post 1ucslyg); five addresses
  and the regions (Zoho's pricing page, checked below); $10 a year for a domain (Vishnu's post).
- **The question it ends on:** the ask, "DM me EMAIL, and I'll send you the exact link". The link stays out of the
  video and the caption.
- **Voice:** Christopher (`voice/settings.json`, as in cohort-pitch-day). Vishnu passed on a clone of his own voice
  on 2026-10-26.
