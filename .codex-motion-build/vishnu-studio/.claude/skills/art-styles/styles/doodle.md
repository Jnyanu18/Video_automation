# Hand-drawn doodle

Notebook paper, handwriting, sketchy boxes, arrows and circles drawn by hand.

Reference tiles: `doodle` in `remotion/src/lanes/styles/StyleSheet.tsx` (a headline, a chat and a stat). Build from them.

- **Colours:** Paper `#fdfdf8` with blue lines `#a9c7ea` and a red margin `#e88`, ink navy `#1d2a4d`, red pen `#e53935`, highlighter `#FFF176`, note fills `#E3F2FD #FFF9C4 #C8E6C9`.
- **Type:** Caveat 700.
- **Shapes and texture:** Wobbly borders (`border-radius: 255px 18px 225px 18px / 18px 225px 18px 255px`), highlighter as a gradient behind the lower half of a word, SVG arrows and circles with round caps.
- **Motion:** Strokes draw themselves on (animate `strokeDashoffset`); handwriting appears word by word.
- **Use for:** Explaining how something works, circling a number, arrows to a point on screen. Vishnu loved it (2026-10-23).
- **Avoid:** Too many doodles at once; a ruler-straight line anywhere.
