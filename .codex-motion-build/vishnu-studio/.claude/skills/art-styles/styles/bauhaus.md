# Bauhaus

The 1920s German art school: circles, half circles, triangles and bars in red, blue and yellow.

Reference tiles: `bauhaus` in `remotion/src/lanes/styles/StyleSheet.tsx` (a headline, a chat and a stat). Build from them.

- **Colours:** Cream `#F2E8D5`, red `#D7263D`, blue `#1D4E9E`, yellow `#F2B705`, black `#111`.
- **Type:** Josefin Sans 700, uppercase.
- **Shapes and texture:** Only geometric shapes in flat colour, thick black bars across the frame, shapes cropped by the edge, the key word on a blue block.
- **Motion:** Shapes slide in on straight lines and turn in quarter turns.
- **Use for:** Structured explanations, three-part ideas, a clean stat.
- **Avoid:** Gradients, shadows, any colour outside the four.
