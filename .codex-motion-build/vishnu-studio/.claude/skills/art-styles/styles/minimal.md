# Minimal monochrome

Black on white, one elegant serif, most of the frame left empty.

Reference tiles: `minimal` in `remotion/src/lanes/styles/StyleSheet.tsx` (a headline, a chat and a stat). Build from them.

- **Colours:** White `#fff`, black `#111`, grey `#999`.
- **Type:** Instrument Serif (italic for the one stressed word), DM Sans 16px spaced caps for labels.
- **Shapes and texture:** Hairline 1px borders, no shadows, no colour, huge margins (56px or more), labels in the corners.
- **Motion:** Slow fades and gentle rises only.
- **Use for:** Serious or emotional lines, quotes, a calm number.
- **Avoid:** Any colour; filling the space.
