# Risograph print

Two or three fluorescent inks printed over each other on rough paper, slightly out of register.

Reference tiles: `riso` in `remotion/src/lanes/styles/StyleSheet.tsx` (a headline, a chat and a stat). Build from them.

- **Colours:** Paper `#F3EEE3`, fluorescent pink `#FF48B0`, blue `#0078BF`, yellow `#FFE800`.
- **Type:** Archivo Black.
- **Shapes and texture:** Ink shapes with `mix-blend-mode: multiply` so overlaps make new colours, a 4 to 6px offset second-ink shadow (misregistration), grain over everything (the `Grain` overlay at 0.45).
- **Motion:** Very little; a slow slide of one ink layer against another.
- **Use for:** Light use only (Vishnu, 2026-10-23): a cover or one carousel now and then, never a regular look.
- **Avoid:** Thin text (it gets lost in the grain); more than three inks.
