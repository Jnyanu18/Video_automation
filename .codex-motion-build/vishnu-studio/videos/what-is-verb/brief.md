# Brief: What Verb is, in one minute

<!-- Stage 0. Written with Claude in conversation before a word of script. -->

- **Brand:** personal (Vishnu). The first of a short Verb series: next, "is it safe to let an AI click
  buttons", then "set it up from Claude Code in one line".
- **Who it is for:** SaaS founders and product teams whose users get lost in menus and ask support how
  to do things the product already does.
- **The one point:** your users should not have to hunt for the button. They ask, and Verb's agent does
  it inside your product, as the signed-in user, with the permissions they already have.
- **The hook (first 5 seconds):** "Your users already know what they want. They just can't find the
  button."
- **Shape and length:** an explainer, about a minute: the problem, what Verb does, a three-step "how a
  product gets it", why it is safe in one line, how to try it. 16:9 only, for YouTube.
- **The metaphor or visual idea:** a maze of menus (Settings > Team > Roles > ...) beside one typed
  sentence that does the same job, ending on the approval card a user sees before anything changes.
- **Real numbers it uses, and where they come from:** all from Verb's own product description (the
  "Verb (landing)" knowledge in the Verb dashboard, read 2026-10-13):
  - free while it launches, no card, and that includes the live site
  - live since 2026-09-30
  - up to 150 actions per site
  - "already live in production at an API company" (Resuelve-API, not named on screen or in the voice
    unless Vishnu has their written permission)
  - setup: a coding agent writes one verb.md file, then one script tag
- **The question it ends on:** "What's the one thing your users keep asking support how to do?"
