# Brief: How long it takes to add an AI agent to your SaaS

- **Brand:** personal
- **Who it is for:** SaaS founders wondering whether an AI agent in their product is a big project; also the
  builders who follow him.
- **The one point:** a read-only AskVerb assistant takes under two minutes; making it do tasks for users is one
  more step (a verb.md file from your coding agent).
- **Purple cow:** a 22-year-old with a 2,500-user app shows an AI agent going into a SaaS in under two minutes;
  shows at: first frame and first words ("I'm 22"); pays off at: the screen run of the quick start.
- **Loops:** (story-loops) A+ "here's how long it takes" / A- "under two minutes", then "under an hour" for actions.
- **Shape and length:** talking head, about 35 seconds, his screen run of the quick start as b-roll.
- **Real numbers, and where each comes from:** 22 (him); more than six apps, one with over 2,500 users (him,
  2026-10-17 to 20); around 100 dollars a month from that app (him, 2026-10-22; currency to confirm); under two
  minutes for the read-only version (him; to be timed on his screen run); day 22 of 30, goal of 10 paying
  customers (the challenge).
- **The question it ends on:** none; it ends on "comment AskVerb and I'll send you the link".
