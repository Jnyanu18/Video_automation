After the worst hurricane in years, one roofer fixed a single house on Palm Street.

Just one.

Before he left, he stuck a QR code on every corner of that roof, facing the street.

"Who scans a roof?" they laughed.

But by morning, every house on the street needed a roofer, and every roofer in town was going to voicemail.

Nobody was picking up.

Then someone looked across the road, saw those little squares, and scanned one.

Then the house next door.

Every scan rang that one roofer's phone, while he was up a ladder with both hands full.

He never touched it.

But every call got answered, and every job on that street was booked before lunch.

So who picked up?

Ava. The AI receptionist built by Auralion Labs, answering his calls while he worked.

Every call. Every job.

Your roof doesn't need QR codes. But your phone needs Ava.

Auralion Labs.
