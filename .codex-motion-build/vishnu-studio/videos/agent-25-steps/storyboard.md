# Storyboard

<!-- One beat per picture change. The first beat starts at 0.
## beat-id | the exact words this beat starts on
chapter: Optional chapter title for the description
What is on screen. -->

16-bit pixel art, black background, gold #ffc400, white, green #2f7a43 for done. Ava (pixel version, to be drawn and
approved first) big at the bottom with a new pose on every beat. A step counter in the top corner is the thread of the
whole video. Example on the card is invented: a delivery moved to a new slot.

## stack | Twenty-five steps across four screens,
chapter: The 25 steps
A tall stack of pixel app screens fills the frame. Counter top corner: 25 STEPS / 4 SCREENS. Ava at the bottom looks up at it. Screens shift on the first word, so the picture changes within 1 s.

## maze | That's what your users do today.
A maze of pixel dialogs, dropdowns and tabs. The counter climbs: STEP 9, 13, 17 / 25. Ava slumped, head down.

## ask | Now picture them typing one sentence instead.
The dialogs vanish. A single text box types "move order 4821 to Friday morning". The counter drops to STEP 1 / 1 in gold. Ava points at the box.

## card | Before anything changes, they see exactly what will change,
chapter: Confirm first
The confirm card slides in, drawn from the real one: eyebrow "Needs your approval", title "Reschedule delivery", rows Order 4821, From Thu 14:00, To Fri 09:00, Customer notified Yes. Buttons Cancel and Confirm. Ava holds up a sign reading CONFIRM.

## confirm | and they press Confirm.
The Confirm button flashes gold and presses in. The card turns green with a check, "Done". Counter reads DONE. Ava thumbs up.

## user | It acts as the signed-in user,
chapter: Safe by design
Four pixel icons in a row, each dim. The first lights gold: a person badge labelled with the user's name. Ava ticks the first box.

## labels | every action is labelled read or write,
The second icon lights: two tags, READ in white, WRITE in gold. Ava ticks the second box.

## log | everything is logged,
The third lights: a scroll of log lines "approved by Eric" ticking up. Ava ticks the third box.

## off | and there's an off switch.
The fourth lights: a big pixel switch flips OFF, the screens behind go dark, then it flips back. Ava ticks the fourth box.

## months | Building that yourself takes months,
chapter: The build
A calendar flips page after page, MONTH 1, 2, 3. Ava at a desk, a pile of screens beside her.

## lock | and making it safe is the hard part.
A pixel padlock drops onto the pile and clicks shut. Ava wipes her forehead.

## cta | Give your app an agent that gets work done.
chapter: Try it
Ava waves. The line in big gold pixel type. A small pixel app window with an agent bubble.

## demo | Try the demo at askverb.com/demos.
askverb.com/demos types in at the bottom. No call ask.

Sound: a library track, chiptune-leaning if one exists (Vishnu picks, no new music). Effects: key clicks on the typed
sentence, a soft whoosh when the dialogs vanish, a chime on Confirm, a switch click on the off switch, a calendar flip,
a lock click. Length about 32 s. Cover options: the stack with 25 STEPS (hook), or Ava next to the Confirm card.
