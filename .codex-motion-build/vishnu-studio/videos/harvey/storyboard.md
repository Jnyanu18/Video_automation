# Storyboard: This Claude agent gets you customers

Look: black, dark liquid glass, Ava big at the bottom acting every line (the engine's standard look), a banner naming each
station, one camera ride between stations. Remade from research/viral/DeghgsivKu7: a character story with a banner per
section, a ticking counter, props that play the line, a new mood per beat. Vishnu, 2026-11-02: how the visuals tell the story
matters more than the content, and the first take (a terminal, no Ava) did not hold a viewer.

Each beat says what happens, to whom, and what changes.

| Time | Station and banner | What happens | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 3.9 | CLAUDE AGENT GETS YOU CUSTOMERS | The customers counter ticks 0 up while people line up under it (marked EXAMPLE) | The hook's promise is on screen from the first frame |
| 3.9 to 8.0 | YOU BRIEF IT, IT HUNTS | Two cards fly in from each side: what you sell, your dream customer; then a radar pulses | You give two facts and it sets off |
| 8.0 to 14.9 | IT PICKS SITES THAT NEED HELP | Five small websites appear, a gold scan sweeps each, three get a red pin and a border, two get a tick and fade | Many become a few, for a visible reason (no booking page) |
| 14.9 to 19.8 | ONE REAL DETAIL PER EMAIL | The email appears; its key sentence is highlighted; a gold line runs from the missing button on Maple Dental's site to that sentence; two more emails slide out behind | The finding becomes the sentence; every email is different |
| 19.8 to 24.0 | IT DRAFTS, YOU APPROVE | Dr. Rivera at Maple Dental replies; the draft appears; the outbox waits; the A key presses and SENT stamps | Tension (waiting) then release (approved), with you in control |
| 24.0 to 29.6 | RUNS ON YOUR CLAUDE PLAN | The Claude plan card; the model API bill stamps $0 | The cost question is answered |
| 29.6 to 31.3 | A DASHBOARD SHOWS IT ALL | The numbers count up (sample data) | Everything above is one pipeline |
| 31.3 to end | COMMENT HARVEY FOR THE GUIDE | A neon HARVEY, the keyword typed, the reply | The call to act |

Through-line: Maple Dental is the first site pinned, the subject of the email, and the sender of the reply.

Ava: present, point-left (the brief), wow (the hunt), think, wink (the pick), point-left, wow (the real detail), think, cheer
(approve), shock (the craziest part), present, wave. Twelve poses.

Sound: ding on the pick, sparkle on the real detail, click then stamp on approve, chime on $0, snap on the reply. Six hits
of ours plus Ava's swishes and one tick on the keyword.

Placeholders: every name, `.test` site, number and email. The counter is labelled EXAMPLE, the dashboard SAMPLE DATA.
