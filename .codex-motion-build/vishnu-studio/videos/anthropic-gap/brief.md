  - The guide's summary: "tell Claude what it can't see, give it a check to run".
  - Goal: "Does it know what done looks like? A goal condition, a spec file, or a failing test it has to turn green."
  - Interview: "Ask Claude to interview you instead: ... Interview me until you could write the spec, then write it to
    SPEC.md." Claude "comes back with multiple-choice questions ... It keeps asking until the answers add up to a spec."
  - Check: give it a check to run (tests, typecheck), and /goal sets a condition Claude keeps working toward.
  - "Most people write one giant prompt" is the reference's framing, said as the common habit, not a measured number.

## Direction

Look: the white keynote (34-three-agents). The structure (Vishnu, 2026-11-06): **the gap is a height**. Anthropic's devs stand
on a high ledge, everyone else stands at the bottom, and each step of the method is a step they climb: GOAL, INTERVIEW,
CHECK. Our own simple dev figures (round heads, a laptop each), never the reference's characters. Claude's mark with the
Anthropic devs. A side view: the ledge on the right, the steps build from the bottom left up to it. The steps are very thin
floating platforms with clear space between them (Vishnu, 2026-11-06), so the dev jumps from one to the next. Each chapter zooms into
its step to show what it is, then pulls back to the climb. Beat table to be timed to the voice:
