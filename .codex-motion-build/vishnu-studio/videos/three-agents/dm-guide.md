# Dots vs Muse vs Grok Bot: the full comparison

Three AI agents launched within weeks of each other in late 2026. They look alike, but they do different jobs. This guide puts them side by side. Everything here is from launch coverage and the companies' own pages, checked on 6 November 2026. Plans and prices change quickly, so check each company's page before you pay.

## The short version

- **OpenAI Dots:** ongoing work. An agent inside ChatGPT that keeps working on a goal between conversations.
- **Meta Muse:** your personal life. An agent that books, fills in forms and shops for you.
- **xAI Grok Bot:** a team. Several always-on bots, each with its own job, that ask you before anything important.

## OpenAI Dots

**What it is:** always-on agents inside ChatGPT. Each dot runs on its own cloud computer with a browser and keeps working between conversations. The dots are a crew of plush mascots, such as a blue cloud, a green frog, a yellow triangle with glasses and a pink heart with sunglasses.

**Launched:** 29 September 2026, at OpenAI DevDay.

**Who can get it:** ChatGPT Pro and Business Premium subscribers in eligible markets, with one dot included. Free, Go and Plus plans do not get one. At launch, Pro users in the EEA, Switzerland and the UK were excluded.

**Strengths:** works in the background on a standing goal; can be reached in Slack and Teams; connects to thousands of apps through OpenAI's plugins.

**Limits:** at launch a dot could not make calls or have its own email address, and background research had read-only access to connected apps. OpenAI says it can make mistakes, so review anything important.

Sources: SiliconANGLE (29 Sep 2026), BetaNews, eesel, TechRadar, Gizmodo.

## Meta Muse

**What it is:** a personal AI agent with a plush mascot called Hatch. You message it in the Muse app or WhatsApp like a person, and it acts for you.

**Launched:** early September 2026, in the US first, on iOS, Android, the web and WhatsApp.

**What it does:** plans and books trips and appointments, fills out online forms with details you give it, finds products and checks out through connected stores (using single-use card numbers through Link by Stripe). Announced store partners include Best Buy, Gap, Sephora, Walmart and Wayfair.

**Pricing:** free within a usage limit, with paid plans at $20 and $100 a month.

**Limits:** reports say it asks for your approval before actions such as sending emails or making purchases. Amazon restricted its buying on Amazon. Meta says it will sometimes make mistakes, and coverage has raised privacy questions.

Sources: TechRepublic, TechCrunch (23 Sep 2026), TechJournal, GuruFocus, AP via PBS NewsHour.

## xAI Grok Bot

**What it is:** a team of always-on agents. Each bot has a computer in the cloud, signs into the tools you already use and works end to end, coming back to you only when something needs your approval. You message bots like teammates, and they can work in parallel in a group chat.

**Launched:** beta on 11 August 2026; Team Bots (shared bots for teams, each with a Slack handle) on 28 September 2026.

**Who can get it:** at launch, SuperGrok, SuperGrok Plus and SuperGrok Heavy, and Cursor Pro, Pro+ and Ultra subscribers, plus Cursor Teams; Enterprise on a waitlist. Reported pricing differs between sources, so check xAI's page.

**Strengths:** made for running several jobs at once: sales outreach, marketing campaigns, operations and bug fixes were the uses xAI's own teams started with.

**Limits:** sources disagree on whether each bot gets its own computer or all of your bots share one; xAI's documentation reportedly says bots should not be treated as a security boundary.

Sources: xAI (x.ai/news/introducing-grok-bot, 11 Aug 2026), VentureBeat, Unite.AI, Data Phoenix.

## Which one is for you

- You want something to keep a project moving while you are away: **Dots** (if you are on ChatGPT Pro or Business Premium).
- You want help with bookings, forms and shopping in your own life: **Muse**.
- You run a small team or a business and want several agents each doing a job: **Grok Bot**.

## Other agents worth a look

- **Claude** (Anthropic): Claude Code and Claude's agent features for work done on your own computer and files.
- **Manus:** a general-purpose agent that runs multi-step tasks in its own cloud computer.
- **Lindy:** no-code agents for email, scheduling and sales workflows.

Check each one's current plans before choosing.
