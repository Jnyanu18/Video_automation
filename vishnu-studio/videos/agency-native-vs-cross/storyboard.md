# Storyboard v1: Your app doesn't need to be built twice

Contact sheet: `stills/contact-sheet.png`, 16 single frames, nothing rendered. 9:16, 1080x1920, 30 fps. The beat table is
in `brief.md` under Direction. Code: `remotion/src/lanes/animated/agency-native-vs-cross.tsx` (self-contained, own captions).

**The timings are estimated** from the script at a normal pace (about 37 s). The real voice (Christopher) will move every
beat a little; the beats are keyed to the spoken words, so they follow the voice automatically. Expected length with the
real voice: about 32 to 36 s.

## Acts

1. 0 to 17 s, ONE CODEBASE: one phone splits into two on "built twice", the shared code card, the old way (two files, two
   bills), "not anymore", React Native and Flutter, the same release on both.
2. 17 to 28.5 s, WHEN TO GO NATIVE: Swift and Kotlin, the camera screen, a chip per piece of hardware, NATIVE stamp.
3. 28.5 to 32.5 s, EVERYTHING ELSE: two phones scrolling an orders list, BUILD ONCE.
4. 32.5 s to the end, FREE CHECKLIST: four questions tick off, Comment STACK.
