## Caption

Most SaaS teams want their app to do the work for the user, not just answer questions. The hard part isn't the AI. It's making it safe: acting as the signed-in user, showing what will change, and keeping a log.

Swipe to see how it works. If you're building something like this, I'd love to hear how you're approaching it.
