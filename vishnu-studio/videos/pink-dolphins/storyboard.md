## hook | this dolphin lives in a river
Dusk over a wide river, jungle silhouettes on both banks. A pink dolphin arcs out of the water.

## pink | it's pink
The dolphin hangs at the top of its arc and glows pink. Word: "PINK".

## born | and it wasn't born that way
The pink drains out of it, top to tail, until it is grey. It splashes back in.

## boto | it's the amazon river dolphin
Underwater. The dolphin swims across. Label: "Amazon river dolphin", small kicker "the boto".

## calves | calves are born gray
A small grey calf swims up beside it.

## size | the biggest males grow
The dolphin beside a standing human outline, a measuring line between them: "2.5 m".

## flood | every rainy season
The water level rises up the tree trunks until the forest stands in the river. The dolphin weaves between trunks.

## neck | its neck bones
Close on the head: it turns sideways, an arc drawn from nose to nose. "90°".

## muddy | the water is too muddy
The water clouds to brown; everything beyond the dolphin's nose fades out.

## sonar | so it hunts with sound
Rings pulse out from the forehead. One hits a hidden fish, which lights up as the echo comes back. "80 clicks / second".

## legend | people on the river tell a story
Night falls. A full moon rises over the river; the water goes silver.

## man | at night
A man's silhouette stands on the bank where the ripples were.

## gone | by morning
Dawn light. The bank is empty; a single ripple spreads on the water.

## why | so why pink
Close on the dolphin, grey. Word: "WHY PINK?"

## scars | scars
Thin scratch lines appear across its skin and glow pink, one by one, until the whole body is pink. Word: "SCARS".

## males | males fight the most
Two dolphins clash in a swirl of bubbles; the pinker one swims out of it.

## endangered | today the
A net drifts down and a dam wall rises across the river. Stamp: "ENDANGERED".

## end | the pinkest ones
The net and wall fade. One pink dolphin swims toward a low sun.
