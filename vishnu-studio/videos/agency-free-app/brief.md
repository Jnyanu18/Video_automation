# Brief: We'll build your app for free

- **Brand:** agency (Auralion Labs). Vishnu to camera, talking-head lane.
- **Who it is for:** founders and people with an app idea who have not built it yet: non-technical founders, small
  business owners, students with a side project.
- **The one point:** comment APP, send your idea, and one idea gets its first version built free. Everyone else gets a
  reply and a free call.
- **Purple cow:** a studio giving away its core product, the build itself. Shows at: the first words ("I'll build the
  first version for free"). Pays off at: "That one's on us", then again at "Nobody gets ignored".
- **Loops:** (story-loops) A+ "I'll build it for free" (the viewer wonders what the catch is) / B+ "Why free?" answered
  at B- "So we're building one" / C+ "Everyone else" (what if I'm not picked?) closed at C- "Nobody gets ignored" /
  A- the call to action.
- **Shape and length:** 9:16, about 55 seconds, eight short paragraphs. First word inside 0.3 s, a visible change by 1 s.
- **Real numbers, and where each comes from:** none. Only facts: Vishnu is a co-founder (site's About page), the studio
  builds apps, websites and AI (site's What we build), Bangalore (About).
- **The question it ends on:** none; it ends on "comment APP".

## The mechanics behind the video (must be ready before it is posted)

- **Comment automation:** keyword APP sends the form link by DM.
- **Google Form:** name, email, phone, the idea, who it is for, anything else. A line on the form saying the details are
  used only to reply about this offer and to follow up about their idea (that consent covers the follow-up to people
  not picked).
- **Rules, written down and linked from the form and the caption:** one idea is picked; what "first version" includes
  (a scope agreed together); what stays with the winner (app store fees, hosting, if so); the closing date and the date
  the pick is announced; how it is picked (by fit, not at random); not sponsored by or associated with Instagram (Meta's
  rule for promotions).
- **Follow-up to everyone else:** a reply from Vishnu and a link to the free 15-minute call (cal.com/auralionlabs/15min).

## Left out on purpose

Prices, client names, "30+ products" and "5+ years" (the site says them; our notes say there is no agency client yet,
so not repeated until Vishnu confirms), "newly opened" (it contradicts the site's numbers; see open questions).
