- **Funnel:** top (jaw-dropping AI news, no guide: ends on a follow)

- **What the reference gets wrong, and we do not repeat:** the model is not called "Hope" (Hope is the architecture it
  builds on); "31% to 48% on new facts" is not in Google's write-up (it reports 48.9% single-task and 46.2% continued
  pre-training on factual knowledge, without baseline scores), so no number is said.
- **Facts and sources (Google Research blog, "The sleep framework: a new way for LLMs to retain knowledge in continual
  learning", Ali Behrouz and Vahab Mirrokni, 2026-10-27; paper arXiv 2606.03979, "Language Models Need Sleep"):**
  - wake phase: inputs stored in fast, short-term memory components;
  - sleep, consolidation: short-term memories moved into newly unlocked low-rank experts in slower blocks "without
    disrupting older parameters";
  - sleep, dreaming: the model generates its own practice examples, randomly activates unusual experts to mix domains,
    keeps the highest-scoring ones, fine-tunes on them and checks whether it improved;
  - Manchu and Kalamang, unseen languages learned in stages: near-zero performance drop, while in-context learning,
    Cartridges and supervised fine-tuning "suffered severe catastrophic forgetting";
  - a research framework; no product or Gemini mention.
