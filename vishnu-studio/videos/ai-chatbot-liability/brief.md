# Brief: Your chatbot's words are your words

- **Brand:** personal (Vishnu). The second single-take video (single.tsx), Ava and Christopher.
- **Who it is for:** founders who added, or are about to add, an AI chatbot or agent to their product.
- **The one point:** whatever your bot says is legally yours, and four lines in its prompt cover the big risks.
- **Where it comes from:** the structure of adilet.fndr's "your vibe-coded app can get sued" reel (DdyqycEINNe):
  everyday thing, real law, real number, escalating, a twist line, a fix, an ask. His list is his; ours is about AI
  chatbots, so nothing in it is his (research/2026-10-26-how-adilet-builds-a-reel.md).
- **The hook (first 2 seconds):** "$5,000" per chat lands as a rolling number on the first line. (The first draft led with
  "fifteen million euros"; Art. 99(6) caps start-ups at the lower of EUR 15M or 3%, so that hook overstated it for this audience.)
- **Shape and length:** hook, four cases rising in money (a small precedent, then $5,000 per chat, $193,000, EUR 15M),
  the twist, four fixes, the ask. About 50 s at 1.12x.
- **The visual idea:** one take, Ava acting every line, a "RISK" tally top right that climbs with each case, a dark
  flood for the twist "you signed", with a signature scrawling itself under the bot's reply.
- **Ask:** DM "AGENT" for the exact four-line prompt.
