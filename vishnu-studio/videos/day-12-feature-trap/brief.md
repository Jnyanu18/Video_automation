# Brief: I hid in the product instead of selling

- **Who it is for:** solo founders building in public, mostly technical, who default to building.
- **The one point:** at zero customers, a new feature is a comfortable way to avoid selling.
- **The hook (first 5 seconds):** "Day twelve of trying to get ten paying customers. And I did the worst thing a founder can do."
- **Shape and length:** a short confession, about a minute; 16:9 and a 9:16 cut.
- **The metaphor or visual idea:** the product as a hiding place; the numbers as the cold daylight.
- **Real numbers it uses, and where they come from:** docs/53 Day 12: 140 invited on LinkedIn, 9 connected, 0 replies, 0 paying.
- **The question it ends on:** what stops you hiding in the product when selling gets uncomfortable?
