AI might have explained why you dream.

Train an AI on the same data again and again, and something breaks. It stops learning the pattern and starts memorising the examples. Show it anything new, and it fails.

Engineers call this overfitting.

In 2021, neuroscientist Erik Hoel asked: what if your brain has the same problem?

You wake in the same room, take the same road, see the same people. Learn only that, and you'd be perfect at yesterday and lost tomorrow.

So every night, your brain makes up things you've never lived. Rooms that bend. Strangers you've never met. Places that don't exist.

It's training data for a life you haven't lived yet.

AI engineers do the same thing on purpose. They add noise and made-up examples so the model stays flexible.

So the next weird dream you have? That's your brain, training.
