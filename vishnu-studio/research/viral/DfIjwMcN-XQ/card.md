## Notes (Claude, from the sheets, 2026-11-09)

- Hook: "Imagine you miss the last train home": second person, the viewer is the character from the first word, and the
  gate slams on "behind you" inside 6 s.
- Shape: 99 s, one continuous dilemma story told as "you". Problem stacked on problem (alone, 3% battery, no signal, next
  train 05:30), then a rule found on a note (NEVER BOARD AN EMPTY TRAIN, the rest torn off), then midnight, lights out, a
  silent empty train, footsteps, breath on your neck. Ends on a choice with two labelled options (1 GET ON / 2 STAY) and
  "So, what would you do?": the comments do the rest.
- Look: 3D render, a faceless white mannequin with drawn cartoon brows and eyes (expressions carry the emotion), a
  realistic grey Underground station, then darkness lit only by headlights; a black twin of the mannequin is the threat.
- Motion: about 20 hard cuts, a new shot every 3 to 5 s: wide, the prop in close-up (phone 23:41 3%, departure board
  05:30, the note, the clock 23:59 to 00:00), the face in close-up for each emotion. Slow camera pushes.
- Captions: two or three words, white heavy caps, centre of frame.
- Sound: 1.3 hits a second, quiet; the tension is the narration and the silence.
- Borrow: "Imagine you", stacked problems, a found rule, a countdown clock, a face close-up per emotion, the two labelled
  choices and the comment question.
- Our twist: the dilemma is one our buyers live (a founder on launch night, an owner whose phone rings mid-job), and
